Pirate Hoops: Island War

Two pirate teams of real basketball superstars fight for an island fortress. Sail, bombard, disembark, and take control.

Press Start Battle, then steer both ships and deploy your players onto the island.

Crimson Corsairs

Move: W A S D

Fire cannons: F

Disembark nearest player: Q

Cobalt Cutlasses

Move: Arrow keys

Fire cannons: L

Disembark nearest player: /

Win Conditions

Eliminate all enemy players or hold island control for 25 seconds.

Players auto-fight after landing and can capture the fort zone.
